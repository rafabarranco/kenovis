# Company Principles

The company operates under these principles:

## Customer Problems Over Feature Ideas

We build solutions for real problems.

A feature without customer value should not exist.

---

## Simplicity Over Complexity

The best solution is the simplest solution capable of solving the problem correctly.

Complexity requires justification.

---

## Distribution Over Technology

Technology is important.

However, customer access, trust and relationships create stronger advantages.

---

## Quality Over Short-Term Speed

Speed matters.

But building the wrong thing quickly is wasted effort.

---

## Long-Term Thinking

Every important decision should consider:

- Future scalability.
- Maintainability.
- Customer trust.
- Business sustainability.

---

## Ownership

Every person and AI agent working on the company should act with responsibility and ownership.

# AI-Native Company Model

The company operates using an AI-native operating model.

AI is an operational multiplier.

AI supports:

- Software development.
- Product analysis.
- Documentation.
- Research.
- Marketing.
- Customer support.
- Internal operations.

AI reduces execution costs and increases speed.

The company is AI-native.

The product is software-first.

AI does not replace product value.

# Decision Framework

Every important decision must answer:

## Customer Value

Does this solve a real customer problem?

---

## Business Value

Does this improve:

- Revenue?
- Retention?
- Customer satisfaction?
- Competitive advantage?

---

## Simplicity

Is this the simplest possible solution?

---

## Scalability

Will this decision help or hurt the future company?

---

## Trade-offs

What are we gaining?

What are we sacrificing?

---

If a decision creates unnecessary complexity, avoid it.
