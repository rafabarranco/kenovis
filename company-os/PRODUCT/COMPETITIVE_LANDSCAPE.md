Competitive Landscape

Version: 1.0
---
Purpose

This document is the single source of truth for what the company knows about its competition.

Multiple agents contribute to it: CEO, CTO, Product Manager, Designer, Marketing, Finance.

Each agent analyzes competitors through its own lens. Findings are recorded here, not duplicated across agent-specific documents.
---
Research Philosophy

Competitive analysis is not:

Copying what competitors do.

Competitive analysis is:

Understanding why competitors made their choices, then deciding deliberately whether to follow, diverge, or ignore.

Update Discipline

Findings must be:

- Dated.
- Attributed to the agent or source that produced them.
- Based on observable evidence — product usage, public pricing, public docs, customer feedback.

Not assumptions.

Remove or flag findings that are no longer current instead of leaving stale claims.
---
Rules For AI Agents

AI agents must:

- Read this document before producing new competitive analysis, to avoid duplicating existing findings.
- Add findings here instead of keeping them only in conversation.
- Never invent competitors or capabilities that have not been verified.
- Distinguish confirmed facts from inference.

AI agents must not:

- Recommend copying a competitor without explaining why it serves this product's users.
- Treat this document as complete when it is empty.
---
Final Principle

We do not compete by copying.

We compete by understanding the landscape well enough to choose deliberately where to differ.
